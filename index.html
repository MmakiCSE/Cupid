Cupid is a connection club for adults arranging their own private company. Members must be 18 or over and verified. Every introduction requires a signed mutual discretion agreement covering plans, cost and boundaries, and either party may end a meeting early with no penalty. Cancellation is free up to 48 hours before, 50% of the deposit is returned up to 24 hours before, and inside 24 hours the deposit is forfeited. Reviews can only be left by members with a completed, paid meeting. Harassment, pressure, sharing screenshots, or contacting someone off-platform before an introduction is confirmed results in removal. Billing appears under a neutral descriptor, profiles are excluded from search engines, and you may request full data deletion at any time.
